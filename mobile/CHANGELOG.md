# Jace Social phone app changelog

## 2.0.0
- Jace Social for phones is now a real app on both Android and iPhone, instead of the website in a frame: faster, smoother, and it looks like your phone.
- Everything is in it: friends, messages (photos and files, reactions, replies, pins, @mentions, bot slash commands), group chats, servers with their channels, voice channels and settings, profiles, your status and settings, bots, and invite links.
- Calls with friends and voice channels, with your camera. On Android you can share your screen too; on iPhone you see other people's screens.
- Notifications for messages, friend requests and calls while the app is in the background.
- Switch between your accounts (and bots you own) without signing in again.
- Updating from 1.x: sign in once more after installing.

## 1.0.0
- First release: Jace Social on Android and iPhone - friends, messages, servers, voice calls and voice channels, with the camera in calls.
- Invite links open in the app: on an invite page, tap **Open in the app**.
- It's the live Jace Social, so new features show up without updating the app.
