# Jace Social for phones

Friends, direct messages, group chats, servers and voice calls, with Minecraft built in. This is the app for Android and iPhone.

- **Friends:** add people by Minecraft or Jace username, and see who's online and what they're playing.
- **Chat:** direct messages, group chats, and servers with text channels, roles, reactions, replies, files and link previews.
- **Voice:** call friends one-to-one or join voice channels, with your camera if you want. Calls work with the desktop app, the web app and Jace Launcher.
- **Invite links open in the app:** on an invite page, tap **Open in the app**.
- **Always up to date:** the app runs the live Jace Social, so new features show up without updating it.

## Installing

- **Android:** download the APK, open it, and allow installing apps from your browser when asked.
- **iPhone:** the `.ipa` isn't on the App Store, so install it with AltStore or Sideloadly. Or skip installing: open https://jace-social.vercel.app/app in Safari and tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

The same account works on the web, in the desktop app, in Jace Launcher, and in game with the Jace Social mod.
