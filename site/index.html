Everything in one place

Like the chat apps you know, with Minecraft built in.

💬

Messages & group chats

Private messages, and group chats with up to 10 people. Rename them, give them a picture, add and remove people.

🏰

Servers

Create a server for your crew with text channels, admins and invite links. Join others with a code.

🟢

Status

Online, Idle, Do Not Disturb or Invisible, plus a custom status with an emoji.

🎮

Rich presence

Friends see what you're playing: the Minecraft version and loader, the server or world, your modpack, and for how long.

🪪

Profiles

A profile picture, display name, pronouns, an about-me, profile color and up to five links.

🔗

Jace + Minecraft

Sign in with a Jace account or your Minecraft account, and link both. Your friends and chats stay with you.

📞

Voice calls

Call friends from Jace Launcher, and answer or hang up in game with the mod.

🌍

Host & join worlds

Host your singleplayer world for friends with roles and permissions. They click Join, with no port forwarding.

Get it

Pick any of these. They all use the same account.

Web

Open jace-social.vercel.app and sign in with Jace.

Desktop app

Windows, macOS and Linux from the releases page. Sign in with Jace or Minecraft, and get notifications even when the window is closed.

Jace Launcher

Jace Launcher has friends and chat built in. Link your Jace account in Settings → Jace Social.

In Minecraft

Install the Jace Social mod (1.20.1 to 26.3, Fabric, NeoForge and Forge) and press J.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, and the code is on GitHub.

Does Jace Social see my Microsoft password or Minecraft token?

No. Minecraft sign-in works like joining a Minecraft server: Mojang confirms the account, and your tokens never leave your computer.

Do I need Minecraft?

No. Sign in with a Jace account to chat with friends and use servers. Link Minecraft any time to show what you're playing.

What does Invisible do?

You look offline to everyone, but you can still use everything.
